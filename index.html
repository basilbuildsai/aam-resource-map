This map gathers the advanced air mobility studies that states and local governments have published, from statewide plans and aviation system plans to MPO, county, city and airport work. It also tracks the eight teams in the FAA eVTOL Integration Pilot Program (eIPP), with their states, partners and routes.

It is a free resource, and everything is open source. The page, its code and the full dataset are on GitHub, and every study links to the publisher's own copy.

      About this map

      This map lists public advanced air mobility (AAM) studies tied to a specific place: a state, a group of states, a regional planning body (MPO, RPO, COG or TPO), a county, a city or an airport. Further down, the page walks through the eight teams the FAA selected on March 9, 2026 for the eVTOL Integration Pilot Program (eIPP), one team at a time, with their partner states, companies, sites and routes.

      Drone (UAS) program documents, such as state DOT drone manuals and drone task force reports, are kept separate from AAM studies. Turn on "Include drone (UAS) documents" under Layers to see them.

      Each study links to the publishing agency's own copy where one is online. Records marked link not checked were found in the literature but their link has not been confirmed.

      Sources
